# AI Track: the test engineer day

Today you test **RetroFlow**, a REST API for StackCraft's retrospectives, before it goes out to 12 teams. You use AI to get there faster, and you check everything it gives you.

## Start here

**Follow the day on the block pages:** https://switchfully.github.io/ai-track-ps-test/

Per block you choose your pair or your track, you see your steps, and you mark them as done. Your progress is saved in your browser, so stay on the same laptop for the whole block.

## What you need

* **Your group's app URL.** Your coach gives it to you. RetroFlow is already running there, there's nothing to install.
* **The course files in this repo:**
  * [`retroflow-qa-brief.md`](retroflow-qa-brief.md): what RetroFlow should do, and what StackCraft expects from you today
  * [`retroflow-api-reference.md`](retroflow-api-reference.md): the endpoints
  * [`RetroflowApiTest.java`](RetroflowApiTest.java): the test suite you inherited, to read, not to run
  * [`stackcraft-qa-standards.md`](stackcraft-qa-standards.md): StackCraft's QA standards, for Block 3
  * [`ai-cheat-sheets/`](ai-cheat-sheets/): how the AI tools work
* **An AI tool and Postman** (or a browser, or curl). Never used Postman? Do [`postman-in-20-minutes.md`](ai-cheat-sheets/postman-in-20-minutes.md) with your partner first.
* **Writing tests in Block 2 without an editor on your laptop?** Use the [RetroFlow tests starter](https://github.com/switchfully/ai-track-ps-test-retroflow-starter): open it as a GitHub Codespace, and Node.js and Playwright are ready. This needs a free GitHub account.

## Sharing your work

Everything your group makes today goes in one shared folder called `retroflow-qa`: a git repo, a shared drive, whatever works for your group. Pairs rotate every block, and every block builds on the files of the one before.

You don't need to fork this repo. You read from it, you don't change it.
